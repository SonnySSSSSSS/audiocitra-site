Ākāśa · coloured reverb

The rooms you remember.

I measured how classic digital reverbs decay and colour the sound, and fitted these to that. Six units, three slots you can chain, wet EQ, presets and A/B. If you grew up on those old racks.. you'll hear it.

What's in here

Fitted to what I measured.

Megha

Halls, rooms, chambers and sheets, 22 programs, all true stereo. Flip on Motion and the tail starts to drift.

22 programs · true stereo · Motion

Guha

14 programs.. halls, rooms and sheets, plus the fun ones: backward, echo and hold.

14 programs

Vayu

A synth-style reverb, with pre-delay, a colour filter and feedback routed back into the reverb. Weird in the best way.

Colour filter · feedback

Dvaya

77 two-effect presets that pair the reverb with delay, modulation, phaser or pitch blocks.

77 presets

Valuka and Adi

Simple little reverbs from the early digital days. Valuka has 100 programs (flanges and choruses in there too), Adi has 64. Lo-fi and charming.

100 + 64 programs

Optional converters

Set Converters to Vintage and you get the word length, word noise, idle floor and DC of old converters.. the grit nobody asked for but everybody misses. It's on Megha (on by default), Valuka and Adi (off by default).

Converters: Off · Vintage

Three slots

Early, Mid and Late each hold any unit, and each feeds the next. Every slot gets pre-delay, width, cuts, shimmer and freeze. After those: a four-band wet EQ, ducking, chorus and a fidelity control, plus presets and A/B.

Early · Mid · Late · A/B
