What it does

Fitted to what was measured.

Megha

Hall, room, chamber and sheet programs, 22 in all, in true stereo. Motion turns on tail modulation.

22 programs · true stereo · Motion

Guha

14 programs, including halls, rooms, sheets, and backward, echo and hold effects.

14 programs

Vayu

A synth-style reverb with pre-delay, a colour filter and feedback routings into the reverb.

Colour filter · feedback

Dvaya

77 two-effect presets that combine reverb with delay, modulation, phaser and pitch blocks.

77 presets

Valuka and Adi

Simple reverbs of the early digital era. Valuka has 100 programs, including flanges and choruses; Adi has 64.

100 + 64 programs

Optional converters

Set Converters to Vintage to add the word length, word noise, idle floor and DC of vintage converters. It is on Megha (on by default), Valuka and Adi (off by default).

Converters: Off · Vintage

Three slots

Early, Mid and Late each hold any unit and feed the next. Each slot has pre-delay, width, cuts, shimmer and freeze. After them: a four-band wet EQ, ducking, chorus and a fidelity control, with presets and A/B.

Early · Mid · Late · A/B
