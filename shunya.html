Shunya · transparent reverb

Clear space, no grain added.

Shunya adds no saturation, drive or converter grain of its own. Six units fitted to the measured behaviour of classic reverbs, three slots you can chain, wet EQ, presets and A/B.

What it does

Six spaces, one clean path.

Mandapa

Hall, plate, room, chamber, ambience and space programs in true stereo, with a Motion control for the tail.

True stereo · Motion

Vistara

Six algorithms: Hall, Random Hall, Plate, Ambience, Rich Plate and Concert Hall, with size, pre-delay and diffusion controls.

6 algorithms

Taranga

11 reverb types, from halls and rooms to a sheet, a tube and a cellar, plus 8 chorus types.

11 reverbs · 8 choruses

Dhvani

Eight types of room, stage, hall and echo, with delay feedback and three chorus types. The chorus can feed the reverb.

Chorus → reverb

Kosha

Chambers, halls, a sheet and echoes, with eight chorus types and a send that feeds the chorus into the reverb.

Chorus → reverb

Chatur

Rooms, sheets and shaped programs, plus Backward Swell and Gate Burst: 10 programs in all.

10 programs

Three slots

Early, Mid and Late each hold any unit and feed the next, so you can chain a room into a hall. After them: a four-band wet EQ, presets and A/B.

Early · Mid · Late · A/B
