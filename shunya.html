Shunya · transparent reverb

Clear space, no grain added.

Ākāśa's quiet sibling. Shunya adds no saturation, drive or converter grain of its own.. just the space. Six units fitted to my analysis of classic reverbs, three slots you can chain, wet EQ, presets and A/B.

What's in here

Six spaces, one clean path.

Mandapa

Hall, plate, room, chamber, ambience and space programs, true stereo, with a Motion control for the tail.

True stereo · Motion

Vistara

Six algorithms: Hall, Random Hall, Plate, Ambience, Rich Plate and Concert Hall. You get size, pre-delay and diffusion to play with.

6 algorithms

Taranga

11 reverb types, from halls and rooms all the way to a sheet, a tube and.. yep, a cellar. Plus 8 chorus types.

11 reverbs · 8 choruses

Dhvani

Eight types of room, stage, hall and echo, with delay feedback and three chorus types. And the chorus can feed into the reverb.

Chorus → reverb

Kosha

Chambers, halls, a sheet and echoes, eight chorus types, and a send that pushes the chorus into the reverb.

Chorus → reverb

Chatur

Rooms, sheets and shaped programs, plus Backward Swell and Gate Burst. 10 programs in all.

10 programs

Three slots

Early, Mid and Late each hold any unit and feed the next, so you can chain a room into a hall.. which I do constantly. After them: a four-band wet EQ, presets and A/B.

Early · Mid · Late · A/B

FIT: Classic or Analyzed

Same nerd switch as Ākāśa. I refitted the late reverb against my analysis with gradient descent, and the FIT switch in the lotus panel flips between Classic and Analyzed with a crossfade. Mandapa also got new edge depths and a refit of its top-end damping.

FIT: Classic · Analyzed
