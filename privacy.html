Privacy

Privacy.

Short version: the plug-ins don't track you. Nothing is sent from them unless you click something, apart from checking for updates.

Who is responsible

Son M Trinh, trading as Audio Citra (New Jersey, USA), is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact support@audiocitra.com.

When you buy

Paddle.com handles checkout as merchant of record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email, country and order details, never your card details. We use them to send your licence, provide support and keep tax records.

The licence

Your licence is a signed file tied to your name or email. It is checked on your computer, offline. There is no activation server and no machine ID.

Update checks

The plug-ins download a signed list of releases from GitHub to see if a newer version exists. No ID or personal data is sent with that request; GitHub sees your IP address as with any download. The plug-in asks before checking automatically; otherwise it checks only when you click Check for updates.

The contact form

The contact page sends your message, your e-mail address and your name if you give it, so I can reply. They are kept with the support request and deleted 180 days after its last activity.

Contacting support from the plug-in

If you click “Contact support”, the plug-in sends your message, your email if you give it, and an optional diagnostics report. You can see exactly what will be sent before sending it. The report holds logs, system details (Windows version, DAW, CPU), the plug-in's settings and parameters, with licence fields removed and your user folder name hidden. It never holds audio, your projects or file listings.

If you switch on “check for replies”, the plug-in asks our support server about your open requests about once an hour. It is off by default.

This website

No ad trackers and no analytics cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. Two pages also load code from other services, which see your IP address: Paddle's checkout script on the buy page, and jsDelivr for the 3D viewer on the Tulā page.

Services we use

support.audiocitra.com handles the contact form, support requests, licences and download links. It runs on Cloudflare, which also stores that data. Plug-in downloads come from GitHub. E-mail, including your licence and support replies, goes out through Resend.

Why we use it

  • Order details and your licence: to deliver what you bought and support it (a contract with you), and to keep tax records (a legal duty).
  • Contact messages and support requests: to answer you (our legitimate interest in replying, or steps you asked for before buying).
  • Rate limits on our servers: to stop abuse. Addresses are kept only in a hashed, short-lived form (legitimate interest in keeping the service safe).
  • Automatic update checks and reply checks in the plug-ins: only if you switch them on (your consent, which you can withdraw by switching them off).

Who receives it

Paddle (checkout, payment and receipts), Cloudflare (the support server and its storage), Resend (e-mail), GitHub (downloads and update checks) and Google Fonts (fonts on this site). Also professional advisers such as an accountant, and authorities when the law requires it. Nobody else, and we never sell it.

I'm in the United States, so your data is handled there, and the services above may process it in other countries.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Paddle's checkout may set its own cookies when you open it; see Paddle's privacy policy.

Keeping it safe

Every connection to the site and the support server uses HTTPS. Support data sits behind a private admin login, and the support server keeps only what this page describes.

How long we keep data

Order records: as long as tax law requires (usually 7 years). Licence records: as long as the licence exists, so it can be resent or reissued. Support requests and contact messages: up to 180 days after the last activity, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct, delete or hand over a copy of it, or to restrict or stop using it, by e-mailing us. You can withdraw any consent at any time. We answer within one month. We do not sell personal data. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
