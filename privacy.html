Privacy

Privacy.

Short version: the plug-ins don't track you. Nothing is sent from them unless you click something, apart from checking for updates.

Who is responsible

Son M Trinh, trading as Audio Citra (New Jersey, USA), contact support@audiocitra.com.

When you buy

Paddle.com handles checkout as merchant of record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email, country and order details, never your card details. We use them to send your licence, provide support and keep tax records.

The licence

Your licence is a signed file tied to your name or email. It is checked on your computer, offline. There is no activation server and no machine ID.

Update checks

The plug-ins download a signed list of releases from GitHub to see if a newer version exists. No ID or personal data is sent with that request; GitHub sees your IP address as with any download. The plug-in asks before checking automatically; otherwise it checks only when you click Check for updates.

Contacting support from the plug-in

If you click “Contact support”, the plug-in sends your message, your email if you give it, and an optional diagnostics report. You can see exactly what will be sent before sending it. The report holds logs, system details (Windows version, DAW, CPU), the plug-in's settings and parameters, with licence fields removed and your user folder name hidden. It never holds audio, your projects or file listings.

If you switch on “check for replies”, the plug-in asks our support server about your open requests about once an hour. It is off by default.

This website

No ad trackers and no analytics cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

How long we keep data

Order records: as long as tax law requires (usually 7 years). Support requests: up to 180 days, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or ask what we hold, by emailing us. We do not sell personal data. If you are in the EU/UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
