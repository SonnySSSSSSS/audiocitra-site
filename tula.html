The smallest thing here, and the one I put on every track first. At default settings it nulls to −158 dB, which is just float rounding.

What's in here

Small box, does the boring stuff right.

Listen

Pick Peak, RMS or LUFS and a Target, hit Listen and play the loudest part. It waits for audio, listens for six seconds and sets Trim for you. LUFS is proper BS.1770, gated and K-weighted, and Peak ignores that one stray click.

Peak · RMS · LUFS · 6 s

Listen All

Start it from any track and every Tulā in the session listens at once and sets its own gain. One pass and your whole session is staged.. that one still makes me happy.

Every linked instance

Link, Match and A/B

With Link on, Out moves opposite to Trim so the track never gets louder on you. Match sets Out so what comes out is as loud as what went in, and A/B flips to the dry signal at that same level. Louder always sounds better, so I took that off the table.

Link · Match · A/B

Pan and width

Pan with four laws: Balance (the default, so inserting it changes nothing), -3, -4.5 and -6 dB. Width goes 0 to 200 % without touching the mono sum, and Mono lets you check the fold-down, with a lamp that shows how much you lose.

4 pan laws · width · mono check

Phase

Give tracks that should line up the same group and hit Align. They listen to 8 seconds of playback, then it finds each track's delay and polarity and delays the early ones to meet the latest. Fix L/R does the same between the two sides of one track, and it's a button on purpose, because on a spaced stereo pair that time difference is the image. Cohere turns the bands below 960 Hz toward the rest of the group, with a meter showing what's left.

Align · Fix L/R · Cohere

Five skins

Studio, Paper, Rack, Console and Citra, all from the lotus menu. They are not recolours: each one has its own shape, layout, type and meter, from a big digital readout to a 2U rack with twin VUs to a lotus that lights petal by petal. Resize it however you like.
