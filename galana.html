What it does

Filters with room to move.

Twelve filter types

Drive, Bite, Comb +, Comb −, Formant, Twin, Wave, Fold, Square, Crush, Ladder and Cascade. Drive, Bite, Twin and Wave offer eight modes: LP, HP and BP at 12 and 24 dB, Notch and All-pass. Bite builds its 24 dB modes from two stacked state-variable stages.

12 types · 8 modes

Movement

An envelope, an LFO and a follower move the cutoff. The LFO runs from a 1/16 note to 2 bars, or free from 0.02 to 20 Hz, in seven shapes including Steps, Glide and S&H. Movement can follow the input or a sidechain.

Envelope · LFO · follower

Comb ±

Crossfade from a feedforward to a feedback comb, with a soft ceiling in the feedback loop.

FF → FB · soft ceiling

Chorus and delay

Chorus has four styles: Classic, Wide, Slow and Fast. The delay is set in note lengths, with feedback, tone and ping-pong.

4 chorus styles · ping-pong

Six stage orders

Filter, Chorus and Delay can run in any of six orders, picked from a list. Oversampling on the filter can be Off, 2x, 4x or 8x.

6 orders · oversampling
