Refunds.

Every plug-in has a free 14-day trial with nothing locked, so you can be sure before you buy. If it still isn't right, we refund.

The policy

You can ask for a full refund within [14] days of purchase, for any reason. Email support@audiocitra.com with your order number, or reply to your receipt email.

How it is paid back

Orders are processed by our reseller, Paddle.com, who is the merchant of record. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Paddle, normally within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

After a refund

The licence for that order stops being valid. Please remove it from your plug-ins; they return to trial-ended mode.

After the refund window

We look at late requests case by case, for example if a plug-in can't run on your system and support can't fix it.

Your legal rights as a consumer, including any statutory withdrawal right where you live, are not affected by this policy.
