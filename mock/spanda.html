This is the big one.. the one I've been building the longest.

Console, rail, neural, dynamics, EQ, finisher and a true-peak limiter. A lot of it I fitted to analysis, then broke back down into parts you can combine yourself. Oh, and strips on different tracks talk to each other.

Plus the everyday stuff: a preset browser that auditions presets live on your sound, Auto Gain (match loudness or peaks), two bypass modes, and licence and updates built right in.
