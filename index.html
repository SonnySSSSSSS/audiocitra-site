Six instruments for mixing, tone and space

Tools for tone and space, played like instruments.

A channel strip, an effects box, a distortion, two reverbs and a modulation box. Many of their behaviours are fitted to measurements, and each is built to respond to your hands. VST3 for Windows.

The plugins

Channel strip

Spanda

Seven modules in one strip, with many behaviours fitted to measurements and parts you can combine.

Effects box

Gālana

Twelve filter types, a Movement LFO, chorus and delay, run in any of six orders.

Distortion

Agni

Two engines that share ten shapes, five routings, a capture slot and cab IRs.

Coloured reverb

Ākāśa

Six reverb units fitted to the decay and colour of classic digital reverbs, with optional vintage converters.

Transparent reverb

Shunya

Six clean reverb units with no saturation, drive or converter grain of their own.

Modulation

Lahari

Chorus, celeste, flanger and phaser in five units, fitted to classic digital chorus sections.
