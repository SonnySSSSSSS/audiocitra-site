## Platform

web

## Users
Home and project-studio producers and mixers on Windows. They're tired of plug-ins that get a unit's behaviour right but never its tone. They try a plug-in for 14 days and buy only what they're already using.

## Product Purpose
audiocitra.com presents and sells Audio Citra's VST3 plug-ins for Windows. Spanda (channel strip), Ākāśa (coloured reverb) and Tulā (mix utility) are on sale in early access. Gālana, Agni, Shunya and Lahari are "coming soon". Success means a visitor understands what a plug-in does and how it sounds, tries it, and keeps it.

## Positioning
**Draft, not settled.** The owner isn't sure what the real difference is, so don't build design or copy decisions on this section until they confirm it.

The owner's own words: a long-time mixer with a lot of gripes, building the plug-ins they wanted and couldn't find. Not a pro studio, and the site never claims to be one. The About page states over two decades of audio production, mixing and mastering, and brief work with two Grammy award winners. The aim is plug-ins that sound like the gear, not just act like it. They're built with AI, said openly, with parts fitted to analysis of real units and then checked by ear.

## Operating Context
- Visitors judge sound: before/after audio clips (`audio/`), a "try it" player with sample stems, measurement figures and 3D renders of each plug-in's skins.
- Buying goes through Paddle, the reseller and merchant of record. The licence is e-mailed, or fetched inside the plug-in.
- Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a VST3 host. Mac isn't supported yet.
- The site is static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages. Every push to `main` deploys to audiocitra.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Every released plug-in runs fully for 14 days. One licence covers all of the buyer's own computers.
- Early access (beta): every update is free, including 1.0 and all 1.x. Refunds are 14 days per plug-in, including each one in the Everything bundle.
- Coming-soon plug-ins have no Download or single-licence Buy until they're released.
- Plug-ins aren't code-signed yet. Walkthrough videos come only after all development is finished.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Audio Citra. Plug-in names keep their diacritics (Ākāśa, Gālana, Tulā).
- Voice: the owner's own, first person, plain and honest, including doubt ("I'm no audio genius"). No marketing speak.
- Logo: the owner's cyan lotus artwork, used only as supplied. Never redraw, retrace or recolour it, and never substitute another mark. A dark backing behind it is the only allowed treatment. The owner's standing rule; ask before any change to the logo.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real assets: before/after audio clips (`audio/`, listed in `audio/clips.json`), renders and screenshots (`img/`), measurement figures on product pages, and the About story.
- None exist yet: customer testimonials, user counts, press, or A/B comparisons against named competitors. Never invent these.

## Product Principles
1. Sound first: let visitors hear it before reading claims about it.
2. Only say what's true and buyable today. Availability, price and policy copy must match what's actually for sale.
3. Speak as one person, not a company.
4. Show the work: measurements and listening, with AI's role stated plainly.
