What it does

Everything between clean and gone.

Two engines, ten shapes

Each engine picks its own shape from the same ten: Soft, Hard, Diode, Fold, Rectify, Tube, Transistor, Germanium, Tape and Crush. Set Drive and Bias; the scope shows the transfer curve as you turn them.

2 engines · 10 shapes

Routings, feedback and modulation

Five ways to wire the two engines, with a feedback loop around the pair. Two modulators (LFO or envelope) can move drive, tone, bias or balance.

5 routings · feedback · 2 mods

Capture and cab

Load a neural capture (.nam) after the engines, with a gate and a tight, mid and bright voicing in front of it. Then blend two cab impulse responses.
